# 03 — Research Questions and Testable Hypotheses

## Primary research question

How can public data and transparent experimental methods improve visibility of AI activity inside broad UK economic product categories while preserving National Accounts concepts and uncertainty?

## Workstream A — Product disaggregation

**RQ-A1:** Which public evidence can credibly inform AI shares within selected CPA groups?  
**RQ-A2:** How different are direct, proportional, modelled and hybrid decompositions?  
**RQ-A3:** Which product groups contribute most to uncertainty in an aggregate experimental scenario?

Hypothesis for testing, not assumption: results for digital-service categories will be more sensitive to classification/revenue-share assumptions than categories where a physical AI-specific product can be more directly identified.

## Workstream B — Business-population identification

**RQ-B1:** Can transparent text features identify AI-relevant business activity with useful precision/recall?  
**RQ-B2:** How much does a rule-based baseline differ from an ML/embedding classifier?  
**RQ-B3:** Which categories require the most human adjudication?

## Workstream C — Asset versus service treatment

**RQ-C1:** Which scenario features most affect whether AI expenditure is considered asset formation, intermediate consumption or an import?  
**RQ-C2:** How do economic ownership and residency change interpretation?

## Workstream D — Adoption versus value

**RQ-D1:** How clearly can the product demonstrate that adoption/intensity indicators do not directly equal economic contribution?  
**RQ-D2:** Which additional monetary measures would bridge the gap?

## Workstream E — Data gaps

**RQ-E1:** Which missing data elements most constrain decomposition?  
**RQ-E2:** Which gaps are addressable with public administrative/alternative data?  
**RQ-E3:** Which require survey redesign or confidential microdata and must therefore remain outside this prototype?

## Research outputs

Each workstream should produce a short research note with question, data, method, finding, uncertainty, limitations and next action. Use `templates/research_note_template.md`.
