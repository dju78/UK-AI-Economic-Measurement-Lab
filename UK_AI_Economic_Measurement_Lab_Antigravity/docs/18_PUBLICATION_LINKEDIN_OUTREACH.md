# 18 — Publication, LinkedIn Launch and Professional Outreach

## 1. Launch principle

Lead with the **measurement problem**, not with “I built a dashboard”. The strongest signal is the combination of economic reasoning, methodology, transparent uncertainty and engineering discipline.

## 2. Required launch assets

- production URL;
- public GitHub repository;
- 60–90 second demo video;
- one-page methodology summary;
- validation report;
- changelog/version;
- 3–5 screenshots;
- short technical research note.

## 3. Suggested LinkedIn structure

**Opening question:** “How do we measure AI’s contribution to the UK economy when AI activity is embedded inside broader products, services and global supply chains?”

Then:
- reference the ONS thematic-account research problem;
- explain that published broad product categories contain AI and non-AI activity;
- state what the lab lets users test;
- emphasise that outputs are independent experimental scenarios, not official statistics;
- show reproducibility/QA;
- invite methodological critique.

## 4. Technical outreach after launch

Once quality gates pass, a concise note can be sent to the ONS Non-Financial Assets Development team listed on the methodology page. The request should be for **technical feedback**, not endorsement or recruitment.

Suggested subject:

`Independent prototype exploring AI thematic-account measurement — request for technical feedback`

Suggested approach:
- 3-sentence context;
- link to live product;
- link to methodology and GitHub;
- identify one or two questions where feedback would be valuable;
- state clearly that it is independent.

## 5. Evidence of professional capability

The project should make these capabilities visible without explicitly boasting:
- statistical methodology;
- National Accounts reasoning;
- data engineering;
- Python/TypeScript development;
- transparent ML;
- QA/reproducibility;
- accessibility;
- communication of uncertainty.
