# 12 — Governance, Ethics, Disclosure and Legal Positioning

## 1. Independence statement

The project is owned and produced independently by Daramola Omoyele. ONS and other public bodies are data/methodology sources only unless a formal collaboration is later agreed.

## 2. Statistical integrity

- distinguish fact, derivation and model output;
- publish caveats near the result, not buried in footer text;
- avoid false precision;
- never remove inconvenient uncertainty for presentation impact;
- publish corrections.

## 3. AI/ML ethics

The classifier can create reputational risk if it labels real businesses incorrectly. Therefore:
- use public information only;
- present classifications as model-assisted and reviewable;
- avoid adverse or sensitive conclusions;
- provide correction/feedback route;
- retain model/version evidence;
- do not use protected personal attributes.

## 4. Privacy

MVP should not need personal data. If public company records contain personal officer information, do not ingest it unless necessary for an explicit research question. Company officers/PSC data are out of scope.

## 5. Licensing

Maintain a source licence field. ONS web content is generally available under the Open Government Licence except where stated; verify source-specific terms before redistribution. Link back to source and attribute appropriately.

## 6. Branding

Do not:
- use ONS logo as project logo;
- make an ONS-like header that could confuse users;
- use `ons.gov.uk` in the project domain;
- say “ONS prototype”.

May say:
- “Inspired by the ONS framework for measuring AI in the UK economy”;
- “Uses published ONS data”;
- “Independent research prototype”.

## 7. External contact

Only contact ONS after a stable public release with methodology, limitations, QA report and source code available. Ask for technical feedback on methodology, not endorsement.
