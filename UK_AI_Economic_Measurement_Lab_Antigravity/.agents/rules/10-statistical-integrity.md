# Statistical Integrity

Recommended activation: **Always On**.

- Broad AI-relevant CPA totals contain AI and non-AI activity. Never call them AI output.
- Distinguish published source values, deterministic derivations and prototype/modelled estimates.
- Every public number needs source, population/scope, reference period, unit, vintage and statistical status.
- Keep raw sources immutable; hash acquired files.
- Keep transformations versioned and reproducible.
- Never convert absence of evidence into a zero without documented source semantics.
- When methodology is ambiguous, expose the ambiguity and record an assumption/decision instead of hiding it.
- No AI-GDP estimate is authorised in Phase 0/1.
