# ONS Source Notes — 21 September 2026

This file captures the specific public facts used to define the build package.

## ONS thematic-account publication

- ONS published the methodology on 21 September 2026 and states that the article sets out its approach to compiling an AI thematic account; the article itself says these are not official statistics. [S1]
- ONS describes a thematic account as a way to provide visibility for an activity by disaggregating/rearranging the integrated National Accounts framework while retaining core principles. [S1]
- ONS identifies 23 broad CPA product categories containing potential AI activity and states these broad estimates need disaggregation into AI and non-AI products. [S1]
- ONS reports an initial list of approximately 5,860 UK-registered AI-relevant companies supplied through DSIT collaboration, while also stating that the list and activity descriptions are experimental and need validation/improvement. [S1]
- ONS lists unresolved problems: product disaggregation, embedded/blurred AI boundaries, AI-business population validation, AI/non-AI revenue separation, alternative data sources and timeliness. [S1]
- Public roadmap: Q4 2026 evidence/data landscape; Q1 2027 methodology options including direct/proportional/modelled/hybrid; Q2–Q4 2027 prototype/test estimates; Q1 2028 experimental account ready to productionise. [S1]

## BICS context

ONS reports AI use among businesses with 10+ employees increasing from around 12% in late 2023 to around 35% in June 2026; adoption differs substantially by industry. ONS also notes adoption measures do not directly solve the value-measurement problem. [S2]

## Product design implications

- do not equate adoption with output/GVA;
- do not equate broad CPA totals with AI activity;
- treat company classification and revenue attribution as separate problems;
- make asset/service/residency/ownership decisions visible;
- show uncertainty and data gaps as product features, not footnotes.
